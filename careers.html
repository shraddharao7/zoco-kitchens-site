Growth

We train on the job, pair newer team members with people who've done it longer, and we'd rather promote from inside the kitchen than hire from outside it. If you want to build a real career in food and hospitality, this is a place that will invest in that.

Working conditions

A kitchen you can be proud of

Clean stations, safe equipment, and an environment where people can focus on doing great work without cutting corners. Hygiene and safety aren't negotiable here: our vegetarian and non-vegetarian prep areas are kept fully separate, ingredient handling follows a strict routine, and we treat kitchen cleanliness as part of the job, not an afterthought.

The process

How hiring works

No long interviews. We hire in the kitchen.

1

WhatsApp us

Name, current or last kitchen, years of experience, and the role. A voice note is fine.

2

Tasting round

Cook two or three dishes: one yours, one from our recipe card. Short and simple.

3

Trial shift

A short shift on station so we can see how you work, and you can see how we run the kitchen.

4

Offer

Clear duties, clear expectations, and a joining date. Referral bonus if a teammate brought you in.
