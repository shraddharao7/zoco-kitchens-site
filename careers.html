Why cook at Zoco

A small team, a serious brand

The menu is engineered, the packaging is designed, the launch is planned — we need the people who'll run the fire.

Founding team, not staff #400

You're chef #1, #2, or #3. Set up the kitchen, shape the SOPs, help hire the people who come after you.

Recipes are ready

Gram-level recipe cards for every dish. Strong grill skills matter more than knowing Mexican food — we'll train the fusion side.

Respect, in practice

Paid trial days. Salary stated upfront. Daily staff meals from our own menu. FoSTaC certification sponsored.

The process

How hiring works

No long interviews. We hire in the kitchen, and we pay for your time.

WhatsApp us

Name, current/last kitchen, years of experience, and the role. A voice note is fine.

Tasting round

Cook 2–3 dishes — one yours, one from our recipe card. Short and simple.

Paid trial day

A full day on station in the kitchen, paid in cash the same day whether or not we proceed.

Offer

Clear salary, clear duties, joining date. Referral thanks if you brought a teammate.
