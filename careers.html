Why cook at Zoco

A small team, a serious brand

The menu's engineered, the packaging's designed, and the launch is planned. We need the people who'll run the fire.

Founding team, not staff #400

You're chef #1, #2, or #3. You'll help set up the kitchen, shape the SOPs, and hire the people who come after you.

Recipes are ready

Every dish has a gram-level recipe card. Strong grill skills matter more than knowing Mexican food already. We'll train you on the fusion side.

Growth that's real

The plan is more outlets. Our founding head chef becomes the lead chef who trains every kitchen that opens after this one.

Respect, in practice

Paid trial days. Salary stated upfront. Daily staff meals from our own menu. We'll sponsor your FoSTaC certification.

The process

How hiring works

No long interviews. We hire in the kitchen, and we pay for your time.

1

WhatsApp us

Name, current or last kitchen, years of experience, and the role. A voice note is fine.

2

Tasting round

Cook two or three dishes: one yours, one from our recipe card. Short and simple.

3

Paid trial day

A full day on station in the kitchen, paid in cash that same day whether or not we proceed.

4

Offer

Clear salary, clear duties, and a joining date. Referral thanks if you brought a teammate.
