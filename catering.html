Zoco Mixers

A lineup of house-made, non-alcoholic mixer bases built around the same Mediterranean and Mexican flavors as the menu. Pour one over ice, top it up, and pair it with whatever's behind your bar.

Zoco supplies the mixer bases only; we're a kitchen, not a bar, so bring your own spirits. Pairing suggestions below are just that, suggestions.

Souk Sour

Citrus · Mediterranean

Fresh lime, orange blossom, and mint, shaken bright.

Pairs well with gin or vodka

Casa Picante

Mexican · Chilli

Tamarind and chilli-lime with a tajín-style salted rim.

Pairs well with tequila or mezcal

Beirut Breeze

Herbs · Mediterranean

Cucumber, mint, and a whisper of rosewater.

Pairs well with gin or white rum

Tropicalia Verde

Tropical fruit · Chilli

Pineapple and mango with a jalapeño kick at the finish.

Pairs well with white rum or tequila

Spice Route Cola

Spices · Mexican

Cinnamon, cardamom, and orange peel over a cola base.

Pairs well with dark rum or whisky

Desert Bloom

Herbs · Mediterranean florals

Hibiscus, rose, and fresh lime, served long over ice.

Pairs well with gin or vodka
