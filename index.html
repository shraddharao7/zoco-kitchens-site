Our story

A souk that moved to Puebla

711–1492

Moorish rule brings Arabic deep into Spanish — zoco, from souk, marketplace, is one of hundreds of words that cross over.

1930s, Puebla

Lebanese immigrants adapt their spit-grilled shawarma to local ingredients. Al pastor is born — Middle Eastern technique, Mexican soul.

Today, Hyderabad

Zoco finishes the story on purpose: one halal kitchen, two cuisines that were quietly the same idea all along.
